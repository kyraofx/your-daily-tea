## Daily Briefing

### Edition Schedule and Window

- Publish one edition daily at **6:00 AM Pacific**.
- Each edition covers **6:00 AM Pacific on the previous day through 5:59 AM Pacific on the edition date**.
- A story published after the cutoff belongs to the following day's edition, regardless of importance.
- Save the completed edition once and serve the same frozen edition to every visitor.
- Preserve prior editions permanently rather than overwriting them.

### Section Order

The website must present sections in this order:

1. Top Stories
2. Tech & AI
3. Jobs, Work & Hiring Trends
4. Science & Planet
5. Health & Wellness
6. Internet & Trends
7. Gaming
8. Life & Society
9. Culture, Entertainment & Pop Culture
10. Money & Economy
11. Politics & World
12. Sports
13. Other Notable News

Top Stories is a ranked presentation of selected category stories, not an independent retrieval category. It is generated after selection from the subject categories and shown first to readers. A promoted story retains its original category and archive metadata.

### Category Coverage

- **Tech & AI:** AI, Big Tech, startups, cybersecurity, product launches, and AI infrastructure.
- **Jobs, Work & Hiring Trends:** hiring, layoffs, internships, salaries, workplace changes, entry-level trends, and AI's effect on work.
- **Science & Planet:** research, space, discoveries, climate, environment, energy, and extreme weather.
- **Health & Wellness:** medicine, public health, nutrition, fitness, mental health, and health research.
- **Internet & Trends:** viral stories, creators, memes, social-media trends, internet debates, and notable internet moments.
- **Gaming:** releases, gaming companies, consoles, studios, industry news, esports, and culturally significant gaming stories.
- **Life & Society:** education, housing, immigration, dating, relationships, demographics, Gen Z trends, and social change.
- **Culture, Entertainment & Pop Culture:** movies, television, music, celebrities, fashion, books, awards, and the entertainment industry.
- **Money & Economy:** markets, inflation, interest rates, housing costs, personal finance, and major economic developments.
- **Politics & World:** U.S. politics, government, courts, elections, wars, diplomacy, and geopolitics.
- **Sports:** major games, championships, records, trades, athletes, and culturally significant sports stories.
- **Other Notable News:** worthwhile stories that genuinely do not fit another category.

### Retrieval and Selection

- Run targeted, category-specific retrieval passes.
- Collect enough candidates to select only worthwhile stories; do not publish a candidate merely because it was retrieved.
- Verify publication timestamps against the edition window.
- Merge duplicate coverage into one story and prefer original reporting.
- Compare candidates with prior archived coverage and require materially new information.
- Treat social and trend signals as discovery inputs, not sufficient factual verification.
- Allow **0–4 stories for most subject sections**. Do not force filler or symmetrical section lengths.
- Choose approximately **3–5 Top Stories** from the stories selected for the full edition.
- Prevent one topic from dominating the edition and perform a final diversity/editorial pass.
- Label opinion or analysis when included.
- Require strong sourcing for extraordinary claims.

### Editorial Scoring

Score candidates on a 0–100 scale using:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Importance | 25% |
| Interestingness | 20% |
| Audience relevance | 20% |
| Newness | 15% |
| Source quality | 15% |
| Momentum | 5% |

The score supports editorial selection; it does not automatically determine publication and does not need to be public.

### Story Presentation

Each published story should include:

- A clear, concrete headline.
- A concise conversational summary, generally 2–4 sentences or an equivalently compact briefing bullet.
- Source attribution and a link to the original reporting.
- Publication date/time.
- Approximately 2–5 relevant topics displayed as hashtags.

The presentation must not include a standard **"Why it matters"** section. Particularly relevant career or field-specific context may be flagged inside a story only when genuinely useful.

Tone may vary by section while facts remain neutral: politics should be clear and neutral; technology smart but accessible; money should explain jargon; entertainment and internet coverage may be lighter; sports may be energetic.

### Archive and Filtering

- Provide a Today view and an Archive view.
- Let readers open an edition by date and see exactly what that morning's edition contained.
- Support previous/next date navigation and a calendar-style date picker.
- Store topics as structured entities and display them as hashtags.
- Clicking a hashtag must show all matching archived stories ordered/grouped by date.
- Topic results must support date filters, including all time, today, last 7 days, last 30 days, and a custom date or date range.
- Category-specific retrieval is a pipeline requirement; category filtering of archived topic results is a future enhancement unless promoted into MVP later.
